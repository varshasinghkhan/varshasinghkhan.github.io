01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGE

Food is everyday.
Understanding it matters.

My approach starts with better questions: what is in our food, what does the evidence say, and how can we make sense of it?

“Nutrition information should empower people — not confuse or overwhelm them.”

I'm building my knowledge in nutrition and health education, with a growing interest in food research, ingredient literacy and evidence-based communication.

Through Nutrition Decoded, I look beyond health claims and popular food trends to explore the science, context and practical details that shape our everyday choices.

This is an educational space. I value credible sources, careful interpretation and clear communication — without one-size-fits-all promises.
